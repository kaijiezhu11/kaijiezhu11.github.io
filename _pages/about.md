I’m a second-year Ph.D student at UCSB, fortunately advised by Prof. [William Wang](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~william/) and Prof. [Wenbo Guo](https://henrygwb.github.io/). I am currently a part-time research intern at AMD Research. Previously, I have interned at Microsoft Research (Redmond, WA) and Microsoft Research Asia, advised by Prof. [Jindong Wang](https://jd92.wang/) and Prof. [Xing Xie](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/xingx/).

My research interests span evaluating, training, and securing AI agent systems. In my spare time, I love playing tennis and Texas hold’em.

- **Agent for Tool Use**: training ([TermiGen, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07274)), benchmarking ([DevOps-Gym, ICLR 2026](https://www.devops-gym.com/)), defending indirect prompt injection ([MELON, ICML 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.05174))
- **Dynamic Evaluation of LLMs**: [DyVal ICLR’24 Spotlight](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=gjfOL9z5Xr), [DyVal 2 ICML’24](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=DwTgy1hXXo), PromptBench (JMLR MLOSS)
- **Adversarial Robustness**: [RiFT ICCV’23](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/papers/Zhu_Improving_Generalization_of_Adversarial_Training_via_Robust_Critical_Fine-Tuning_ICCV_2023_paper.pdf), [PromptRobust CCS’24 LAMPS](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3689217.3690621)
